Quarterly Results 3Q26 | Gerdau S.A. 3 MESSAGE FROM MANAGEMENT The third quarter of 2026 marked another step in the Company's profitability expansion. We recorded an Adjusted EBITDA of R$3.8 billion, up 9.9% from 2Q26 and 37.8% higher than 3Q25, along with an Adjusted EBITDA margin of 20.2%, both at their highest levels since the second quarter of 2023. This performance was mainly driven by improved realized prices in North America, which more than offset rising costs, while shipment volumes remained virtually stable compared to the previous quarter. With regard to the Company’s reportable segments, once again North America operations remained among the quarter’s key highlights, accounting for more than 76% of the Company’s consolidated EBITDA, reaching R$2.9 billion, with an EBITDA margin of 27.7% (the highest level over the last four years). Market conditions remained favorable throughout the quarter, bolstered by robust demand and an order backlog that continued well above historical averages. This scenario enabled the Company to capture the price adjustments implemented throughout the year, thus boosting profitability despite weaker volumes due to scheduled maintenance shutdowns in Canada. In Brazil, the quarter was marked by a continued competitive environment characterized by a high supply of steel products in some segments. Despite this context, Adjusted EBITDA reached R$741 million, up 5.2% from 2Q26. This result mainly reflected an improved sales mix, with exports accounting for a smaller share of total shipments. While this contributed to higher production costs, it also led to increased realized prices. We achieved shipment growth in the domestic market, particularly in the flat and special steel segments, in addition to price increases in certain product lines. In South America, Adjusted EBITDA totaled R$160 million, down 22.0% from 2Q26. Despite shipment growth at Peru operations, which hit record steel production and shipments in August, the quarter’s results were impacted by lower steel production and higher costs resulting from scheduled maintenance shutdowns, mainly concentrated in Argentina. In addition, the market remained challenging due to weak demand in Argentina and labor disputes in the civil construction sector in Uruguay. We remain committed to enhancing the Company’s competitiveness. In 3Q26, we invested a total of R$955 million in CAPEX, totaling R$3.1 billion in 9M26. As a major operational milestone, ourrecycling facility unit began its operations in Pindamonhangaba. The project expands our metal supply capacity, bolstering our competitiveness and underscoring our commitment to sustainability. Over the next few quarters, we will advance the ramp-up process, while seeking to consolidate our performance and realize the expected benefits. In addition, we are moving into the final phase of integrated testing for the Miguel Burnier sustainable mining platform, which is scheduled to begin operations in 4Q26. We remain disciplined in our capital allocation and shareholder returns. Based on our 3Q26 results, we approved a cash dividend of R$0.34 per share, totaling R$667.0 million, and continued to execute the 2026 Share Buyback Program, investing R$344.1 million to date and reaching 32% of the authorized shares. With 125 years of history, we remain firmly committed to enhancing the Company’s competitiveness, swiftly adapting to market changes, and generating a positive impact in the regions where we operate. We would like to thank our employees, customers, suppliers, partners, shareholders and other stakeholders for their trust and support in building our history and continuously creating value. In Brazil, Gerdau also produces flat steel and iron ore. Dedicated to empowering individuals who shape the future, the Company is a benchmark for internationalization in the Brazilian industrial sector. It is present in several countries in the Americas and relies on 30,000 employees across all its operations. Gerdau has 28 steel production units, including 13 industrial units in North America. Recognized as the largest recycler in Latin America, Gerdau utilizes scrap as a significant raw material, with nearly 70% of its steel production derived from scrap. Annually, it transforms 10 million tonnes of scrap into a diverse range of steel products. As a result of its sustainable production matrix, Gerdau currently has one of the lowest average greenhouse gas (CO₂e) emissions, accounting for half the global average for the sector.
